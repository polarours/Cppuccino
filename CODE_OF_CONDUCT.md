# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Cppuccino is a learning project. We want readers and contributors of every
background, language, and experience level to be able to take part without
harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise about code and generous about people.
- Critique the claim, the example, or the explanation — not the person.
- Assume a question is asked in good faith, especially when English is not the
  author's first language.
- Credit sources, prior art, and other people's examples.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no for scope, accuracy, or maintenance
  reasons, and explain why.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Dismissing a report of misconduct or retaliating against the reporter.

## Scope

This applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
any other space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer by email at
[polarours@zohomail.com](mailto:polarours@zohomail.com), or through the contact
method on [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/polarours). Do
not open a public issue for a conduct report.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly as possible. The maintainer will keep the
reporter's identity confidential, explain the decision to the people involved,
and take proportionate action — from a private warning to a temporary or
permanent ban from project spaces.
